02 / BRING YOUR OWN DATA

What should I test next?

Start with the right material. This model learned grid textures, so a random photograph will not give a meaningful inspection.

⌗

Capture a new mesh photo

This is how to try an image that was not in the benchmark.

  1. Use a close-up of similar woven wire mesh. Fill the frame with the texture.
  2. Keep scale, viewpoint and focus similar to the examples. Use steady, even lighting.
  3. Square framing works best. The entire image is resized to 256 × 256; small details can be lost.
  4. Upload the image, compare all three views, and inspect the material yourself.

PNG / JPEG / WebP · ≤12 MiB · 32–4096 px per side · ≤16 MP. Transparent areas are composited on white. Uploads never update the model.

05 / LOOK UNDER THE HOOD

How does an autoencoder find an anomaly?

It learns to rebuild normal textures. Differences between an image and its reconstruction become a signal to investigate.

01 / BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Learn normal patterns

During training, an encoder compresses normal mesh images into a small representation. A decoder learns to rebuild them. This project trains its own weights from scratch.

Training happens offline
02 / WHEN YOU CHOOSE AN IMAGE

Reconstruct the input

Your image becomes a 256 × 256 RGB input. The frozen model rebuilds it in this browser. Uploading a photo runs inference; it does not retrain the model.

No image upload to a server
03 / WHEN YOU READ THE RESULT

Compare with a fixed limit

Compare pixels with the reconstruction, average the largest 1% of errors, and check the score against 0.02402208. Above the limit suggests review; below it can still miss a defect.

A score, not a probability
What exactly goes into the score?

For each pixel, average squared red, green and blue reconstruction errors. Take the largest 656 of 65,536 pixel errors (the top 1%, rounded up) and average them. Recommend review only when the score is strictly greater than 0.02402208368407517. The relative color map does not change the score or threshold.

Can I use this for any object?

No. This checkpoint learned the MVTec Grid texture domain. Faces, documents, other materials, and images with different scale or lighting are outside the demonstrated use case.

Is the camera a production system?

It is a live browser demonstration that samples one frame at a time. Delay starts after the previous inspection completes. Camera hardware, line speed, defect coverage and end-to-end latency need separate qualification.

What is saved on my device?

Up to 100 result records, including the filename, score, decision, times and model version. Images are not saved in history. Export CSV or download the current JSON record; clear history when finished.

Why can a defective image look “within range”?

An autoencoder can rebuild a defect well enough to produce a low score, or resizing can obscure small defects. The fixed limit also trades false alarms against misses. Use the known benchmark label to spot failures.
